# Déclaration UE de conformité — AIHM v1.0

**Issued under**: Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 — Artificial Intelligence Act,
Articles 47–48.

---

1. **Product / system**:
   AIHM (AI Hiring Manager), version 1.0
   Unique identifier: AIHM-V1.0-2026-06

2. **Name and address of the provider**:
   AIHM SAS — [address pending registration]
   France

3. **This declaration of conformity is issued under the sole responsibility
   of the provider**.

4. **Object of the declaration**:
   A multi-tenant SaaS system that assists HR teams in:
   - Automated CV scoring against a job description
   - Outbound AI-conducted phone screening interviews
   - Generation of recruiter scorecards

   Classified as a **high-risk AI system** under Annex III §4(a) of
   Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (employment, workers management and access
   to self-employment).

     judicial contexts (out of scope; explicitly forbidden by Terms)
